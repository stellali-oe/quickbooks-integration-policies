# Internal Use Terms

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

This is a private, single-user connection between an AI assistant and the account holder's QuickBooks Online company. It is not sold or offered to third parties.

The integration may read accounting information and, when the account holder authorises the relevant action, create, update, void or delete records within available QuickBooks API permissions.

The account holder remains responsible for reviewing accounting changes, financial reports and tax treatment. AI-generated outputs may contain inaccuracies and are not a substitute for professional advice.

Operation depends on QuickBooks Online, Intuit authorisation, and third-party integration and AI services. The account holder can revoke access or discontinue use. This integration is not endorsed by Intuit.
