# Disconnect QuickBooks Online

This is an information page for a private, single-user integration operated locally through the Intuit CLI and DeepSeek Harness.

To disconnect, the account holder should sign out or clear the saved authentication in the local Intuit CLI and revoke the integration's QuickBooks or Intuit connected-app authorisation where available. Check the Intuit or QuickBooks account settings for connected app access.

This webpage does not itself revoke authorisation, erase tokens, or delete accounting records. Revoking access does not necessarily delete data already retained by the AI or other service providers.
