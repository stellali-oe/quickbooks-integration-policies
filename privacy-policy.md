# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

This page describes a private single-user integration between Harness and QuickBooks Online for the account holder's own accounting administration. It is not a public product.

The integration may retrieve company details, accounting records, customers, suppliers, invoices, bills, transactions, and reports as authorised through Intuit. These records may contain personal information.

Data is used for requested accounting assistance, reports, and authorised changes. Task-related content may be processed by the selected AI provider and integration services according to their applicable privacy terms and configuration. This integration is not intended to sell data or publish financial records.

Authorisation uses Intuit OAuth. Secrets, tokens, and accounting records must not be committed to this public repository. Exact credential storage, AI data retention, logging, and approval controls depend on the software configuration and must be verified before live use.

The account holder can revoke the connection through QuickBooks or Intuit connected-app settings. Revocation does not necessarily remove data previously processed by other services. Retention may be governed by accounting obligations and providers' terms.

For questions or corrections, contact the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/stellali-oe/quickbooks-integration-policies).
