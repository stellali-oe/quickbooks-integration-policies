# Connect or Reconnect QuickBooks Online

This is an information page for a private, single-user QuickBooks Online integration operated locally through the Intuit CLI and DeepSeek Harness.

To connect or reconnect, the authorised account holder opens the locally installed Intuit CLI, configures the appropriate Intuit OAuth credentials, and starts the QuickBooks OAuth login flow. The account holder then completes authorisation on Intuit's own sign-in and consent screens.

Connection is not initiated by this webpage. This static page does not process OAuth callbacks, handle credentials, or receive financial data. The actual production OAuth redirect URI must be registered and configured separately in the Intuit Developer Portal.

Access should be authorised only for the intended QuickBooks company and scopes.
